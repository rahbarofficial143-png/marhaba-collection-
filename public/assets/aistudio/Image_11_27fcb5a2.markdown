## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier multi-vendor fashion marketplace tailored to modern ethnic and contemporary Indian apparel. It bridges the exhaustive marketplace density, product discovery, and value-signaling architecture of platforms like Meesho with the artisanal prestige, editorial grace, and curated warmth of a heritage luxury house.

The emotional tone balances regal warmth and commercial clarity:
- **Heritage Credibility**: Rich deep burgundy tones and polished brass metallic accents evoke royal textiles, zardozi embroidery, and festive Indian couture.
- **High-Velocity Utility**: Uncluttered data layouts, explicit discount tiering, transparent vendor provenance badges, and clean transactional structures ensure zero friction across millions of SKU permutations.
- **Editorial Poise**: High-contrast serif headlines paired with precise geometric sans-serif structural elements prevent visual cheapening, even in promotional-heavy views.

The design philosophy combines **Warm Minimalist Editorialism** with **Commercial Dense Utility**. Generous imagery framing coexists with tight, information-rich meta clusters (ratings, dispatch timing, fabric authenticity seals, and tiered Rupee pricing).

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic Indian dye-work, precious metals, and unbleached silk:

- **Primary (`#6B1426` / `#80182E`)**: Royal Crimson & Deep Burgundy. Used for primary calls-to-action, core brand iconography, header banners, and high-priority states.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27` / `#D4A373`)**: Warm Brass & Antique Gold. Applied to trust seals, artisan vendor verification badges, star rating highlights, and subtle borders framing luxury collections.
- **Tertiary (`#80182E`)**: Vibrant Maroon. Deployed for urgent promotional tags, flash drops, and deal badges without resorting to generic high-saturation safety reds.
- **Neutrals & Canvas**:
  - Base Background: Warm Cream (`#FAF8F5`) gives natural woven-textile warmth, replacing cold blue-whites.
  - Surface Containers: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure product cutouts and catalog photography remain uncompromised.
  - Typography & Keylines: Deep Slate (`#0F172A` and `#1E293B`) for crisp, high-contrast legibility, with muted slate tints for secondary metadata.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the editorial gravitas of **Playfair Display** for brand storytelling and collection titles with the utilitarian clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for commerce engines.

- **Editorial Serifs**: Playfair Display is reserved strictly for high-level anchors (H1–H3, collection showcases, artisan highlights). It must never be set in all-caps or used for functional UI labels.
- **Precision Sans-Serif**: Plus Jakarta Sans handles product names, complex specifications (e.g., fabric blends, stitch type, blouse piece inclusions), filter facet lists, and vendor data.
- **Pricing & Currency Formats**: Prices always pair standard numeric bolding with explicit Rupee glyphs (`₹`). Strikethrough MRPs are rendered in `body-sm` neutral muted tints, with percentage reductions set in bold burgundy `label-md`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a disciplined, fluid 12-column grid on desktop screens, scaling down to a 4-column system on mobile devices to preserve high browsing density.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (2-column infinite feed, 8px gutters)
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (3-to-4 column product tiles, 16px gutters)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (4-to-5 column product grid, 16px gutters, sticky facet rail)
- **Marketplace Density Management**: Product listings optimize viewport economy by prioritizing high aspect-ratio imagery (3:4 or 4:5 vertical cuts common to Indian ethnic garments) with compact, zero-gap visual cards. Horizontal scroll rails are used for rapid deal category switching, vendor hubs, and festive flash edits.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows, relying instead on clean surface layering, structural hairline frames, and subtle warm tints:

- **Tonal Layers**: The canvas uses Warm Cream (`#FAF8F5`). Cards sit directly atop this surface in Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`), outlined by delicate warm hairline borders (`1px solid #EFEAE2`).
- **Ambient Warm Shadows**: Critical overlays (sticky bottom checkout sheets, mega menus, quick-view modals) cast low-opacity, warm-tinted shadows: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`.
- **Badge Floating**: Quick-add icons, wishlist toggles, and deal percentage pills float directly over product photos using subtle backdrop filtration: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a `4px` blur.

## Shapes

This design system standardizes on a **Soft** shape scale (Level 1). Minimal radii preserve an upscale, high-fashion catalog discipline without the aggressive corners of unrefined brutalism or the playful informality of bubbly pill interfaces.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Interactive controls, inputs, quick-filter chips, and product image containers.
- **Large Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Modals, category drawer panels, and cart summary containers.
- **Micro Badges**: Trust tags and deal flags retain precise, crisp 2px or 4px radii to match numeric and currency clarity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Burgundy background (`#6B1426`), Crisp White text, 4px radius, medium bold weight. Hover transitions to `#80182E`.
- **Secondary**: Pure White background, Warm Brass border (`1px solid #C59B27`), Charcoal text. Hover fills with a pale brass wash.
- **Buy Now / High Intent**: High-contrast, full-width block buttons in checkout states.

### Filter Chips & Quick Facets
- Horizontal scrolling chips on mobile. Inactive states feature Crisp White cards with muted slate borders. Active states use Burgundy backgrounds or Brass outlines with bold text.
- Meesho-style immediate pill filters include: "Under ₹499", "Pure Silk", "Ready to Ship", "Free Delivery", and "4.0★+".

### Product Listing Cards (PLP)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 or 4:5 image container with a delicate border.
- **Badging**: Deal badge in Deep Burgundy/Maroon sits top-left (`-45% OFF`). Wishlist heart floats top-right with a translucent frosted-glass backdrop.
- **Meta Hierarchy**: 
  1. Vendor provenance / Trust badge (e.g., "Surat Direct", "Verified Artisan") in Brass (`#C59B27`) uppercase micro-text.
  2. Product title in truncated single-line `title-sm`.
  3. Pricing row: Large bold Rupee value (`₹1,249`), struck-through MRP (`₹2,499`), and discount percentage.
  4. Delivery note & rating badge: Green rating pill (`4.2 ★`) next to "Free Delivery by Tomorrow".

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat Crisp White containers with subtle borders (`#E2DCD5`). Focused state shifts border color to Deep Burgundy with a 1px ring. Labels stay fixed above or shift smoothly via floating transition.

### Vendor Trust & Deal Banners
- Styled with subtle heritage motif borders (fine brass lines) featuring verified vendor ratings, return window guarantees, and direct-from-weaver authenticity marks.
